# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what's best for the project and community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior deemed inappropriate.
